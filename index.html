Map & API Configuration

The app is currently running in Interactive Demo Mode (Zero Key Required) using high-res OpenStreetMap tiles and the official UK Government Land Registry API.

When provided, the app activates the official Google Maps SDK (with Satellite view, 45° imagery, and Google Places autocomplete).

Data Sources:
• HM Land Registry: Official Price Paid Data (Open Government Licence v3.0)
• MHCLG EPC Register: Energy Performance Certificates, certified m² floor space & rooms
• VOA 1991 Valuation: Council Tax Band statutory bands and regional local authority averages
• Postcodes.io: UK Postcode & Coordinate reverse geocoding
